## 1. Issue Resolutions

### BG-0001 — Unable to recover correct route after returning to junction
* **Category**: navigation
* **Severity**: high
* **Initial Status**: OPEN → **Final Status**: FIXED
* **Root Cause**: In `src/main.js` junction classification loop, returning to a junction hub along a branch checked `delta < -8` to reset `isOnWrongRoute` to `false`. However, distance along a branch is measured forward from the junction node, so `delta` on the branch side is positive (`delta >= 0`). `delta < -8` could never be satisfied while driving back along the branch, preventing `navigationState.isOnWrongRoute` from resetting to `false` when returning to the junction hub.
* **Fix Implemented**: Updated return-to-junction check in `src/main.js` to evaluate `Math.abs(delta) <= 8 && reverseAligned`. When the cart turns around and drives back within 8 meters of the junction node, `navigationState.currentRouteId` resets to `junction.incomingRouteId` and `isOnWrongRoute` resets to `false`, allowing the player/tester to select and commit to the correct outgoing route.
